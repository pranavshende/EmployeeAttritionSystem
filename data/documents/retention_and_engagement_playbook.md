# People Operations Playbook: High Flight-Risk Employee Retention Interventions

## Section 1: Flight-Risk Diagnostic Protocol
Employees with a Flight Risk Score of 4 or higher (out of 8 criteria) are placed on the Confidential Flight Risk Watchlist. Key trigger indicators include: Overtime without equity participation, compensation below department median, tenure under 2 years, and low job or environment satisfaction ratings.

## Section 2: 5-Day Stay Interview Action Plan
1. **Day 1 - Trigger Notification:** HR Business Partner notifies the VP of the respective Department.
2. **Day 3 - Stay Interview:** Conduct an empathetic 1-on-1 Stay Interview focusing on career trajectory, compensation alignment, and blockers.
3. **Day 5 - Retention Offer:** Where feasible, present adjustments including equity tier elevation, project re-scoping, or flexible work schedule.

## Section 3: Department Retention Benchmarks
Target annualized attrition across Sales, R&D, and Support is capped at 12%. Departments exceeding 18% turnover undergo a mandatory leadership review.
