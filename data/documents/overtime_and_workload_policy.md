# Global Workforce Policy: Overtime, Workload & Burnout Prevention

## Section 1: Overtime Authorization & Workload Limits
1. Standard working hours are 40 hours per workweek (Monday to Friday, 8 hours/day).
2. All scheduled overtime exceeding 5 hours/week must be pre-approved by the Department Director.
3. Consecutive overtime across more than two consecutive pay periods triggers a mandatory Work-Life Balance review by People Operations.

## Section 2: Compensation & Comp-Time Credit
1. Eligible hourly and non-exempt staff receive 1.5x standard hourly rate for overtime hours.
2. Salaried exempt employees working sustained surge periods qualify for Compensatory Time Off (Comp-Time) at 1:1 ratio, up to 10 days per calendar year.

## Section 3: High Flight-Risk Overtime Mitigation
HR Analytics identifies OverTime as a primary flight-risk driver. When an employee is flagged with Overtime and low Job Satisfaction, their manager must redistribute project tasks within 10 business days.
