# Compensation, Equity & Stock Option Tier Framework

## Section 1: Stock Option Equity Tiers
Stock Option Levels range from 0 to 3:
• Level 0: Baseline entry (no equity grant). High attrition correlation observed among Level 0 cohorts.
• Level 1: Standard professional grant (4-year vesting schedule, 1-year cliff).
• Level 2: Senior specialist and leadership grant.
• Level 3: Executive and key retention grant.

## Section 2: Compensation Equity Reviews
Annual salary reviews occur in Q1 and Q3. Employees in the lowest salary quartile (Tier 1) with High Performance ratings qualify for off-cycle adjustments up to 15%.
